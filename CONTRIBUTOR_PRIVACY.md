# Contributor CLA Privacy Notice

## Controller and purpose

EffortlessMetrics, acting through the maintainers of `hl7v2-rs`, controls the contributor licensing records described here. The records are collected only to verify assent to the repository's Contributor License Agreement, administer contribution eligibility, and preserve evidence of the rights granted with accepted contributions.

## Data collected

The hosted CLA Assistant service may record:

- authenticated GitHub account and GitHub user ID;
- full legal name;
- email address;
- the required individual-capacity and authority acknowledgement;
- signature time;
- repository and pull-request context;
- the agreement version or Gist revision presented for signature; and
- service audit metadata needed to verify the signing event.

The project does not ask contributors to re-enter a GitHub username or signature date.

## Service and access

The signing flow is provided by the hosted CLA Assistant GitHub App operated through `cla-assistant.io`. Licensing records are available to authorized project maintainers and to the service operator as needed to provide the signing service. They are not collected for marketing.

## Retention

The copyright and patent grants in the CLA are intended to remain effective while accepted Contributions continue to be used or distributed. The project therefore retains licensing evidence for the life of the Project and afterward for as long as reasonably needed to establish, administer, or defend those rights. Private signature-register exports are retained independently of the hosted service.

## Access and correction

To request access to or correction of a contributor licensing record, email `security@effortlessmetrics.com` with the subject **CLA privacy request: hl7v2-rs**. Do not put a legal name, email address, PHI, or other private signing data in a public issue.

A correction may be stored as a supplemental record rather than rewriting the historical signing event. Records may be retained where they remain necessary to document the license for a Contribution already accepted or distributed.
